How it works

Three steps. No learning curve.

Designed so a grandparent, a student walking home late, or someone in a rural area can use it on the worst day of their life — without thinking.

1

Add your trusted contacts

Choose up to five people — family, a neighbour, a friend. Add their names and numbers once during setup. Takes about a minute.

2

Press the matching button

Medical, fire, intruder, GBV, robbery or kidnapping. Large, clearly labelled buttons — no menus to dig through, no typing under stress.

3

Tap send — that's it

Your SMS app opens with every message already written, including your address and an optional GPS map link. Works on cellular signal alone.

Questions

Straight answers

Everything people ask before trusting an app with their safety.

Will I need internet or mobile data for this app to work?

No. Scream4U works completely offline. Emergency alerts are sent as SMS directly from your phone — no internet required. You only need internet to download the app from Google Play and to subscribe through Google Play. Once set up, every emergency feature works offline.

How do I add emergency contacts?

Go to Settings → Edit Emergency Contacts. You can add up to five contacts with their names and phone numbers. Include country codes for international numbers (for example +1 for the US, +27 for South Africa).

Why are the emergency buttons locked?

Emergency alert buttons require an active subscription. Only the Test Alert is free. Subscribe through Google Play to unlock all six emergency alert types.

How do I test that it actually works?

Use the green Test Alert button on the main screen (or in Settings if you're subscribed). Free users can send three test alerts to confirm the system works. Subscribers get unlimited testing. The test message clearly states that it is not a real emergency.

What is included in an emergency alert?

Each alert contains your name, your home address, GPS coordinates (if location permission is enabled), the type of emergency, the exact timestamp, and a request for your contacts to assist immediately.

Does the app track my location in the background?

No. Scream4U does not track your location. GPS is entirely optional and is only read at the moment you press an emergency button — never in the background.

During setup you may grant location permission. If enabled, your coordinates are added to the SMS as a Google Maps link. GPS uses satellites, so it works without internet. You can turn it on or off any time in Settings → Location Permission. If GPS fails or is disabled, the SMS still sends with your home address.

Your coordinates are retrieved once on your phone, inserted into the message, and then discarded. Scream4U never sees, stores or transmits them, because we cannot access your phone's native SMS app — everything happens locally on your device.

Can I move my subscription to a new phone?

Yes. You'll need an internet connection on the new device. Enter your activation code and the app transfers the subscription, deactivating the old device. Codes are valid for 365 days and work on one device at a time. You can transfer up to three times per year — email support@scream4u.icu if you need more.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription Status → Manage Subscription, or email support@scream4u.icu. We'll process the cancellation promptly and confirm it with you.

What if I press an emergency button by accident?

You get one chance to stop it. The app opens your SMS app with the messages pre-filled — simply close it or don't tap Send, and nothing is delivered.

How are the SMS messages actually sent?

When you press an emergency button, the app opens your phone's SMS app with pre-written messages for all your contacts. You review them and tap Send. This is why it works offline — it only needs cellular signal for SMS delivery.

Why does my phone ask "WhatsApp or SMS"?

Your phone needs a default SMS app set. Go to Settings → Apps → Default apps → SMS app and choose your messaging app. That way alerts send immediately without a prompt.

WhatsApp can't message several contacts at once and needs internet, so Scream4U uses SMS — the most reliable option when networks are down.

How do I subscribe and receive my activation code?

Subscriptions will be handled through Google Play once the app is listed. You'll subscribe inside the app and it unlocks automatically.

Is there an iPhone or iOS version?

Scream4U is Android only. There are currently no plans for an iOS version.

Can I use it if I witness someone else in danger?

Absolutely. If you see a robbery, fire, assault or any emergency happening to someone else, press the matching button to alert your contacts. Your message includes your location and the emergency type, so help can reach them while you stay at a safe distance.

Is my data sold or shared?

No. There is no background tracking, no location history and no data selling. Alerts are composed on your device and sent through your own SMS app. Read the full privacy policy.
